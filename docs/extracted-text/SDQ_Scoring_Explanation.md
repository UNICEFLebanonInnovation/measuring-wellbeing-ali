- **Scale= (average of Matching Theme/question)\*5+0.5**

- **NA question is omitted**

- Example:  
  Emotional scale= Average of (Somatic symptoms, Worries, Unhappy, Nervous in new situations, Many fears)\*5+0.5  
  In case one of the matching questions is NA then the question is omitted from the average.  
  For example, if unhappy is NA them it’s not included in the average. Emotional scale= Average of (Somatic symptoms, Worries, Nervous in new situations, Many fears)\*5+0.5

- **Total Score = SUM of emotional scale, conduct scale, Hyperactivity scale, and problems scale**

- **Pro social scale is not included in the total score**

- **Only applications with post-test are included**

- **Drop-out applications are omitted from the Analysis change**
